Achievements

Our historical timeline, joint ventures, and international standards verification that highlight our role as a trusted industry supplier.

Our Timeline

2005

Company Inception

SSP Industries was established in Bangalore, Karnataka, as a specialized corrugation and industrial paper supply partner for domestic markets.

2010

Magadi Unit A Operations

Commissioned and built Unit A in Magadi Industrial Area, introducing automated cleaning divisions and high-throughput corrugation setups.

2015

Strategic JV with ICAR & ITMU

Signed joint venture agreements with ICAR and ITMU (Government of India) for scientific formulation transfers and biological science integrations.

2020

NABL Laboratory Collaboration

Established NABL-certified partnerships for advanced product testing, ensuring every batch meets international tensile and chemical metrics.

Present

Category-A Global Supplier

Recognized as an A-category B2B supplier to global enterprise giants, delivering eco-friendly, ISO 9001:2015 compliant solutions at scale.
