Pioneering Industrial Synergy

From manufacturing durability to chemical purity and animal nutrition, we construct the vital systems that power global enterprise.

Our Journey Since 2005

Founded in Zurich in 2005, SSP Industries began with a single vision: to elevate the quality standards of packaging materials. Over the last two decades, we recognized the synergistic needs of our corporate clients, leading to expansion into advanced chemical hygiene formulations and organic bio-nutrition feeds.

Today, our vertically integrated divisions allow us to source sustainably, manufacture at scale, and deliver with unparalleled supply chain speed. We serve thousands of corporate customers across 15+ countries, maintaining carbon-neutral goals and zero wastewater records.
